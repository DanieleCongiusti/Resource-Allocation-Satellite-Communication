\section{Define the objectives}
We had to evaluate the performance of the overall system in terms of throughput for various values of \emph{N} and \emph{K}, with \emph{B} uniformly distributed, as the specifications 
provided. We decided to analyze another metric, very important in communication systems: the average queue length at the terminals. To collect this data, we added an \emph{Oracle} module to which
each terminal sends the total bytes sent and the size of its queue length after every time frame.
